Instead, it is an executive summit focused on strategic leadership, ROI-driven business case studies, practical enterprise tool adoption, and bilateral talent connections.
SHIFT is curated specifically for business founders, C-suite executives, senior product leaders, university students in management and computer science, and young professionals aiming to navigate the business frontier of AI rather than just model training.
The event takes place on Saturday, 28 November 2026 in Tunis, Tunisia. The verified executive venue (between Cité de la Culture and UTICA headquarters) will be finalized and communicated to registered ticket holders 30 days in advance.

General executive delegate tickets and partner exhibition credentials involve an access fee that covers summit catering, executive lounge materials, and session recordings.
